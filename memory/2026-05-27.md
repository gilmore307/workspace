## ETF-as-target Layer 2 design discussion

- Chentong raised the future requirement to model ETFs as targets, specifically `QQQ` and sector ETFs such as `XLE`.
- Working design conclusion: Layer 2 should not mean only "the stock's sector"; it should become an explicit `target_context_basket` layer with reviewed context routing.
- Proposed routing fields: `target_symbol`, `target_asset_type`, `layer2_context_symbol` or composite id, `context_relation`, and `cross_state_policy`.
- For sector ETF targets such as `XLE`, use `context_relation = self_context` and `cross_state_policy = disable_self_relative`; Layer 2 can represent the energy basket state, but Layer 3 must not compute fake `XLE vs XLE` residual/confirmation features.
- For broad/style/theme ETF targets such as `QQQ`, use `context_relation = composite_context` with an explicitly reviewed peer/proxy composite such as mega-cap growth / Nasdaq-100 / tech-growth exposure. Do not force QQQ into one GICS sector and do not blindly use QQQ itself as independent context.
- Safe fallback when a reviewed ETF context composite does not exist: `context_relation = no_independent_context`, `layer2_context = null`, and `cross_state_policy = market_only`; this is weaker but preferable to fabricated sector semantics.

## Multi-context target handling gap

- Current Layer 2/3 handling for a target that belongs to multiple baskets is flattened to one primary context.
- `source_02_target_candidate_holdings` can record multiple ETF/sector memberships for a target, but Layer 3 candidate building deduplicates by `(available_time, routing_symbol)` and keeps only the first matched context as the actual model input.
- Other matched contexts are retained only in `metadata_payload_json.collapsed_source_refs` for audit; they do not enter `sector_context_projection_vector`.
- This means targets such as `NVDA` do not currently get simultaneous `NVDA vs XLK`, `NVDA vs SMH`, and weighted-composite context features.
- Future preferred direction: keep one candidate per target/time, but carry `primary_context_symbol`, `secondary_context_symbols`, `context_weights`, and a `context_basket_projection_vector` so Layer 3 can model primary, secondary, weighted-composite, and context-dispersion evidence explicitly.
